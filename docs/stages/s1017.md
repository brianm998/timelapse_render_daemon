# Stage s1017: TRD-09 EXIF collection and sidecars

Summary: `trd-core` collects the EXIF histogram of a raw sequence with batched `exiftool -csv -@` runs (64 files a batch, at most `max_processes.exiftool` at once), writes the sidecar atomically with the `exif_cache` fallback, reads the histogram back (sidecar first, then cache), and gives ffmpeg its single-valued tags.
